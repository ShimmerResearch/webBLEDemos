DFU Flow

Step 1 Enable DFU Service Nordic DFU Service: — not connected —

Use this once to enable Nordic DFU service advertisement after the next disconnect. This does not reboot the device immediately.

Step 2 Reboot To DFU Bootloader Mode

After reconnecting with Nordic DFU service active, use the buttonless DFU command to reboot directly into bootloader mode.

Step 3 Perform Firmware Update

Select a firmware .zip package, then click Begin Update. A browser device picker will open and the DFU library will connect to the bootloader and flash the firmware.

⚠︎ A device is currently connected — disconnect from it first before using the DFU Update Tool.
